Gas safety

  • Keep the cylinder upright and away from heat.
  • Switch the regulator off at night and when away.
  • Check hose and regulator for cracks every few months.
  • Never use a leaking cylinder; open windows and avoid switches.

Smell gas?

Do not light a flame or switch on lights. Open doors and windows, turn off the regulator, move outside and call us or the LPG emergency line 1906.
